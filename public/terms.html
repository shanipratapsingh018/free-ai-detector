What the score means

The AI-likeness score is a rule-based estimate drawn from writing-style signals. It cannot establish who or what wrote a text, and it is sometimes wrong in both directions. Do not use it as the sole basis for any academic, employment, disciplinary or legal decision about a person.

Rewriting tools

Rewrites are suggestions. Offline rules and language models can change meaning, tone or facts, so compare every result with your original before using it. There is no promise that a rewritten text will be rated as human-written by this or any other detector.
