TRANSPARENCY

A useful lens. Never a verdict.

No tool can prove who wrote a text. This one shows its working so you can judge the score for yourself.

How the score is built

The detector measures nine writing-style signals: stock phrasing, sentence rhythm, formulaic openers, personal voice, lists of three, trailing “-ing” clauses, punctuation variety, short sentences and paragraph uniformity. A weighted combination is mapped to a 0–100 scale. It is a rule-based estimate, not a trained classifier.

Where it goes wrong

Formal, academic or heavily edited human writing can score high. Model output that has been edited by a person can score low. Short passages and languages other than English carry less evidence, so their scores are pulled toward 50 and marked low confidence.

How rewriting works

With no setup, rewriting uses offline rules in your browser: plainer wording, tidier mechanics and key-sentence summaries. When the site owner connects a language model, rewrites are produced by that model and checked so numbers and links are not altered.

Do not use this score alone for academic, hiring or disciplinary decisions.

WORTH KNOWING

Questions and answers.

Can it tell for certain that a text was written by AI?

No. The score reflects how closely the writing matches patterns common in model output. It is evidence to weigh, with a stated confidence level, and it is sometimes wrong in both directions.

Is my text uploaded?

Scoring, file reading and offline rewriting all happen in your browser, and nothing is saved. Only a model-backed rewrite sends your passage to this site’s server and on to the model provider, and the tool tells you when that mode is active.

Will humanized text pass other AI detectors?

There is no such promise. The humanizer aims for clearer, more natural writing. Detectors disagree with each other, and none is reliable enough to guarantee an outcome.

Which languages and files are supported?

Any language can be scored and counted correctly, including Hindi and other Indic scripts, but the wording signals are tuned for English. You can open .txt, .md and .docx files up to 2 MB. PDF is not supported yet; copy the text out and paste it.

What are the limits?

Scoring accepts up to 30,000 characters. Rewriting accepts 3 to 1,500 words and up to 12,000 characters per request.

Is it really free?

Yes. Scoring and offline rewriting cost nothing to run. Model-backed rewriting, when enabled, is paid for by the site owner.
